SOP: IT Security Policy

Document ID: SOP-SEC-02 | XITM

Key Policy Points

  • Access control: Only authorised personnel are allowed to access facility system and data, higher ranked data and access only may be accessed by higher ranked personnel.
  • Password management: Employees must use strong passwords, change them regularly and not be shared to unauthorised personnel.
  • Data protection: Sensitive information must be encrypted during transmission.
  • Network security: Firewalls, antivirus software and intrusion detection systems must be maintained and updated.
  • Device usage: Company devices should not be used for personnel activities that could compromise security of the network.
  • Email and internet use: Avoid opening suspicious links or attachments form untrusted sources, follow acceptable use standards.
  • Software installation: Only approved software may be installed on company systems.
  • Incident reporting: All security breaches or suspicious activities must be reported immediately to IT management for next action and recorded.
  • Backup and recovery: Regular backups must be performed and tested to ensure data integrity.
  • Physical security: Server rooms and network equipment must be secured and accessible only to authorised staff.
  • Compliance: All staff must adhere to relevant data protection laws of the organization (e.g. POPIA of South Africa).
  • Bring-your-own-device (BYOD): Unauthorised devices are not allowed to connect to organisation network, may be allowed to connect to guest network if available.
  • Security awareness: All personnel must attend basic network security training for all employees.

SOP: Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Document ID: SOP-AUP-03 | XITM

Key Policy Points

Authorised use

  • Users must use company resources for industry-related activities.
  • Personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with organisation duties or violate policies.

Prohibited Activities

  • Access to unauthorised systems or data.
  • Share passwords with others.
  • Download unauthorised resources.
  • Use organisational resources for illegal activities.
  • Send offensive or discriminatory harassment messages.
  • Violate intellectual or trademark laws.

Data security

  • Users must protect confidential information.
  • Sensitive data must only be shared with authorized personnel.
  • Devices must be locked when unattended.

Internet & email usage

  • Internet access must be used responsibly.
  • Email communication should be professional and industry appropriate.
  • Suspicious emails or attachments should be reported to IT immediately.

Monitoring

  • The organisation reserves the right to monitor the use of its systems and networks to ensure compliance with policy.

Violations

  • Failure to comply with these policies may result in disciplinary action, suspension, or legal action where applicable.

SOP: IT Acquisition Procedures & Approvals Policy

Document ID: SOP-ACQ-04 | XITM

Key Policy Points

  • Policy Purpose & Scope: Standardizes procurement of all IT hardware, software, and cabling across the main administrative block and three lab buildings.
  • Technical Standards Compliance: All purchases must match facility specifications, including Cat6 copper cabling, OM3/OM4 fiber, Layer 2/3 managed switches supporting RSTP, and Gigabit NICs.
  • Step 1 — Requisition Submission: Department leads submit an IT Requisition Form detailing equipment specs, target building/floor, quantity, and operational justification.
  • Step 2 — Technical Review (Network Administrator): Verifies hardware compatibility, port availability on 24-port floor switches, IP address availability on the central server, and VLAN requirements.
  • Step 3 — Management Approval (IT Manager): Evaluates requisitions against allocated IT budgets. Formally approves compliant requests or rejects non-standard equipment with written feedback.
  • Step 4 — Purchasing & Emergency Protocol: Approved requisitions are routed to certified vendors. Critical hardware failures bypass standard wait times via expedited emergency authorization.
  • Step 5 — Asset Logging & Configuration: Incoming hardware is assigned a facility Asset Tag, recorded in the central inventory database, and pre-configured before deployment.

Compliance & Enforcement

  • Unauthorized hardware or software purchased outside this process is strictly prohibited from connecting to the facility network.

SOP: Service Help Desk Procedures

Document ID: SOP-HD-05 | XITM

Key Policy Points

  • Ticketing logging: Record user issues in the help desk ticket system, include details such as the user's contact information and description.
  • Ticket prioritization: Assign prioritisation level based on the impact of the issue e.g.: low, medium, high.
  • Initial diagnosis: Perform initial troubleshooting steps to diagnose the issue, gather information, and determine the cause.
  • Ticket assignment: Assign ticket of issue to the appropriate technician or team based on expertise of issue.
  • Resolution sign-off: resolve the issue according to standard procedure. Document the solution in ticket system and close ticket once user confirms issue is solved.
  • Follow-up: Conduct follow-up communication to ensure the user is satisfied with the resolution, reopen the ticket if additional issues are reported.
  • Escalation procedure: Escalate unresolved or complex issues to higher-level support teams or superiors. Provide detailed documentation of all troubleshooting steps taken.
  • Knowledge base maintenance: Update and maintain a knowledge base of common issues and solutions. Use this resource to improve efficiency and reduce troubleshooting in the future.

SOP: Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity Plan

Document ID: SOP-DR-06 | XITM

Key Policy Points

  • Backup Protocols: Automated daily incremental backups and weekly full backups of the central Research Centre Management database.
  • Failover Actions: Step-by-step restoration protocols for core L3 switches, redundant link failover, and server image deployment.
  • Recovery Time Objective (RTO): Core research database restoration targeted within 2 hours of critical hardware failure.
  • Emergency Team Roles: Defines immediate actions for Network Administrator and IT Manager during catastrophic power loss or network disruption.
